## MSc thesis

This thesis investigates estimating virtual fractional flow reserve from coronary artery geometry using deep learning. It develops a point-cloud approach that combines explicit vessel features with learned geometric representations and evaluates its predictions against reference flow simulations.

The thesis was completed in 2023 under the supervision of Maciej Zięba. The public arXiv version was deposited in 2024. The related CenterlinePointNet++ conference paper is listed as a separate publication.
