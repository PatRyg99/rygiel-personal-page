## AI in vascular decision-making

This chapter examines three vascular-care questions: anticipating aneurysm growth, detecting endoleaks, and estimating the risk of spinal cord ischemia. It discusses how imaging and other patient data can inform computational approaches, alongside practical considerations for their use.

The chapter appears in the “The challenge of artificial intelligence” section of *Technological leaps in vascular surgery: A 2024 overview of what is expected to revolutionize historical paradigms*, edited by Gaetano Lanza and Andrea Kahlberg for SICVE.
