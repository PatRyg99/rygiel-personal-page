## Neural segmentation in a clinical imaging study

Fully convolutional networks are used to delineate white-matter tracts from diffusion tensor imaging. The segmented tracts provide a consistent basis for measuring fractional anisotropy and comparing patients with matched controls.

The study investigates regional changes in white-matter integrity and their association with rheumatological measurements. The article appeared online in December 2025 and belongs to the 2026 journal volume.
