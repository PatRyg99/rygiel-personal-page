## Consistent outcomes for vascular research

Differences in outcome reporting make studies difficult to compare. This work combines a literature review, patient input, a European Delphi survey, and an expert meeting to establish ten core outcomes for AAA research. The resulting set is intended to support both clinical studies and research on AI for disease progression.

Patryk contributed as a member of the VASCUL-AID and Expert Consensus Meeting collaborator group. The named authors and collaborators are recorded separately to preserve the publication’s attribution.
