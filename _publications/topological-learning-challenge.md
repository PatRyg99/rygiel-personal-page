## Beyond pairwise connections

The challenge focuses on topological liftings: transformations that map data into representations such as hypergraphs and cell, simplicial, or combinatorial complexes. Such representations can describe relationships beyond the pairwise edges of a graph.

This report presents the challenge design and summarizes contributions from the participating community. The arXiv record identifies the report with the GRaM workshop at ICML 2024.
