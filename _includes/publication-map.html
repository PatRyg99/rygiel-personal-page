    Affiliations, sources & counting

    Counts cover the {{ affiliations.papers.size }} papers and book chapters with verified affiliations, including preprints and credited consortium contributions. All authors’ affiliations are included. A paper counts once per country and once per institution, regardless of how many authors list it. Departments and campus locations are grouped under their parent institution; separately named research centres are retained. The country totals stay the same when searching or filtering the publication list.

    Affiliations come from the linked paper versions. Where a country is omitted, the named institution’s location is used. Datasets and the single-author thesis are excluded. The ICML Topological Deep Learning Challenge report is also excluded because it lists contributor roles without institutional affiliations.
